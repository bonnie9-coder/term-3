Key Wave Concepts

Transverse Waves

In transverse waves, the particle displacement is perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation. Examples include light waves and ripples on water.

Longitudinal Waves

In longitudinal waves, the particle displacement is parallel to the direction of wave propagation. Examples include sound waves and ultrasound.
