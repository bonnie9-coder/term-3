Key Wave Concepts

Transverse vs. Longitudinal Waves

Transverse: Particle movement is perpendicular to wave energy direction (e.g., Light waves, water waves).

Longitudinal: Particle movement is parallel to wave energy direction (e.g., Sound waves, compression waves).

Refraction & Snell's Law

When light passes from one medium to another (e.g., Air to Glass), its speed changes, causing the ray to bend toward or away from the normal line.
